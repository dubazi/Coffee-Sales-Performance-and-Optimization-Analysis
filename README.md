# BrightLight Retail Sales Performance and Optimization

## Project Overview

This project presents a data-driven sales analysis for a single product at BrightLight Retail, focused on calculating Key Performance
Indicators (KPIs), measuring profitability, and deeply investigating price elastisticity to define optimal promotional strategies.

The core objective was to move beyond simple revenue tracking and provide actionable recommendations for managing discount depth and 
driving sustained profit growth.

## Key Insights
The nalysis yielded several critical findings for BrightLight Retail:
High Price Sensitivity (Elasticity): Product demand is highly elastic, meaning customers are very reactive to price changes. Sales drop sharply as prices increase, particularly after the R70 price point.




Optimal Promotional Sweet Spot: The ideal range for promotions that maximizes sales volume while avoiding sharp drops is R30–R60. Products priced below R50 performed best during promotions, driving the highest quantities sold.



### Profitability & Margin Management:

The average gross profit margin is approximately 40%.

There is a clear trade-off between volume and profitability, as noticeable margin dips occur during promotion periods.

Profit margin generally improved post Week 10 as pricing stabilized.

## Project Deliverables

Daily Sales Overview: Visualizing daily sales fluctuations (R5K–R25K) and the relationship between avg unit price and total sales value.



Weekly/Monthly Performance: Identifying peak revenue weeks (e.g., Week 6, Week 10, Week 14) and observing demand seasonality.



Elasticity Analysis: A scatter plot demonstrating the relationship between price and quantity sold, which proves the elastic nature of demand.

## Recommendations for Optimization:

Optimize promotions by running smaller, targeted discounts.

Monitor profit per unit during discounts to balance volume vs. margin.

Suggested automation via SQL pipelines and dashboarding with Power BI or Looker Studio for ongoing KPI monitoring.
